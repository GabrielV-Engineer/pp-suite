# Brief — Todo App

> **Illustrative example.** This project does not exist. It shows what the pp-suite contract looks
> like for a small project. Names such as `example-owner` stand in for a real person.

## Problem

One person wants a private, fast task list in the browser, without accounts, subscriptions or
sync services.

## Who it is for

A single owner. No sharing, no roles.

## What good looks like

The four quality attributes in `blueprint.yaml`, each with a number: responsiveness (QA-001),
no cross-component coupling (QA-002), no lost tasks (QA-003), safe handling of text (QA-004).

## What it deliberately is not

See `non_goals` in `blueprint.yaml`. They are the scope fence: a slice that seems to need one of
them is a blueprint defect and goes back as a change request.
